Discovery Lens

Paste an explanation written by an AI tool. Discovery Lens flags analogies that may plant a misconception, rewrites the explanation so students infer the idea instead of receiving it, and offers three ways into the same content.

Paste AI-generated content only. Do not paste student work, names or any student information.

Used to place the lesson sequence in your progression. If left empty, the tool uses NGSS or Common Core and says so.

In one sentence

Wording to watch

Analogies, image-laden words, everyday words with a technical meaning, overgeneralizations and facts stated without their conditions: what students might wrongly take away, and a safer wording.

Discovery version

The same content as a sequence of clues and questions, so students build the conclusion themselves.

    Where students should land

    Three ways in

    Let students choose how to start. Each door leads to the same idea, and each ends with a question for them to answer.

    Lesson sequence

    Where the lesson fits in the curriculum, then several activities: from a question to the idea, with what students do, what you do, and how you know each step worked.
